# Flood Access Loss

1. Do not enter moving or unknown-depth floodwater to preserve a planned route.
2. Move the operational plan to higher/safer access points before routes close if officials advise doing so.
3. Treat closed, barricaded, or flooded roads as unavailable in the route workspace.
4. Separate potentially contaminated items from clean supplies after water intrusion.
5. Document water level and access changes in the Field Observation Log.
6. Re-entry happens only when authorities and site conditions indicate it is safe.
