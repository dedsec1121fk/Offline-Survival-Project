# Offline Information Verification

1. Record the exact source, time, and wording of an important instruction.
2. Separate official alerts, firsthand observations, and unverified reports in your notes.
3. Do not forward a claim simply because it matches what you expected to happen.
4. When two credible instructions conflict, prefer the competent authority for the specific hazard and location.
5. Keep stale information visibly timestamped so it is not mistaken for a current instruction.
6. After connectivity returns, correct any operational notes that were based on information later shown to be wrong.
