# Medication Continuity Pack

1. Maintain a current medication list with the exact label/prescriber instructions; the project does not calculate doses.
2. Track on-hand quantity and refill/review date before disruption begins.
3. Include storage requirements and the contact route for the prescribing/dispensing service where appropriate.
4. Keep the emergency medical card concise enough to print or show quickly.
5. Do not substitute, split, stop, or change medication based on this project; use qualified clinical/pharmacy guidance.
6. After any disruption, reconcile what was actually used with the continuity register.
