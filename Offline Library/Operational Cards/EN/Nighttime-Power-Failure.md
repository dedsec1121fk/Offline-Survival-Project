# Nighttime Power Failure

1. Stabilize safe lighting first so movement around the home does not create an avoidable injury.
2. Confirm whether the outage is internal or grid-wide without touching damaged electrical equipment.
3. Move phones and radios to the communications power budget instead of charging everything at once.
4. Assign one person to track official updates while others handle household tasks.
5. Keep combustion devices outdoors and away from openings according to manufacturer and fire-safety guidance.
6. Before sleep, decide which loads stay off, who checks conditions, and when the next status review occurs.
