# Earthquake Aftershock Plan

1. After shaking stops, check immediate hazards before moving through damaged areas.
2. Expect aftershocks and keep routes away from visibly unstable objects or structures.
3. Use the accountability board to identify who is present, away, or not yet reached.
4. If utilities appear damaged, follow utility/emergency guidance rather than improvising repairs.
5. Keep shoes, lighting, communications, and essential supplies accessible during the aftershock period.
6. Record major damage observations with time and location for later handoff.
