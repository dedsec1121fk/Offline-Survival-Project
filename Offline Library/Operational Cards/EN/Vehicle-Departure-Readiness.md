# Vehicle Departure Readiness

1. Check fuel/charge, tires, lights, fluids or other manufacturer-recommended basics before an emergency period when possible.
2. Keep the vehicle route and alternate route in the offline route workspace.
3. Load people, animals, accessibility equipment, medicines, and priority documents before optional cargo.
4. Use the range calculator as a planning check only; reserve fuel/charge for detours and delays.
5. Do not bypass road closures, evacuation controls, or unsafe conditions to stay on a planned route.
6. Record the vehicle, occupants, departure time, and destination in the incident log.
