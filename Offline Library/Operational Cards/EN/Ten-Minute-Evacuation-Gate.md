# Ten-Minute Evacuation Gate

1. Use official evacuation instructions as the primary trigger; do not delay an ordered evacuation to complete a checklist.
2. Account for every person, dependent, and animal before splitting tasks.
3. Take the already-prepared grab-first set rather than searching for low-priority items.
4. Confirm primary and alternate routes against current official information when available.
5. Send one concise check-in to the designated contact if communications are working.
6. Mark departure time and destination in the Incident Log so later coordination has a reliable reference.
