# Digital Device Continuity

1. Decide which device is primary for alerts, which is backup, and which can remain powered off as reserve.
2. Keep offline copies of essential documents and references in formats you have actually tested.
3. Verify charging cables, adapters, power banks, and storage media during maintenance cycles.
4. Use low-power mode and batch synchronization when power is limited.
5. Protect backups that contain personal plans; a backup is useful only if it can be safely restored.
6. After major edits, export a fresh backup and verify it opens before deleting older known-good copies.
