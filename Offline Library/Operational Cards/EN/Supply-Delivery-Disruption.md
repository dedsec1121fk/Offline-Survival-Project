# Supply Delivery Disruption

1. Identify which supplies are truly time-critical versus merely convenient.
2. Measure current stock and actual burn rate before buying or rationing based on guesswork.
3. Set a minimum reserve in the Resource Endurance board for each critical consumable.
4. Find safe substitutes for single points of dependency before stock reaches the reserve line.
5. Prioritize accessibility, medical continuity, sanitation, water, food safety, and communications dependencies.
6. After supply resumes, rebuild the reserve gradually and document what failed first.
