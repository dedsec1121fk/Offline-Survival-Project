# Heat and Power Outage

1. Identify the coolest safe available location before indoor temperatures rise further.
2. Reduce nonessential electrical loads so stored energy can support communications and priority cooling equipment.
3. Use official heat warnings and local cooling-center information when available.
4. Track water stock and actual use without turning a planning estimate into medical advice.
5. Check higher-risk household members more frequently according to their care plan.
6. Escalate to qualified medical/emergency help for concerning heat-related symptoms.
