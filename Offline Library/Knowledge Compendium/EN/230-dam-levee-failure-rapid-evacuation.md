# Dam or Levee Failure: Rapid Evacuation and High-Ground Decisions

A dam or levee failure can create a fast-moving flood with little time for household optimization. The useful offline plan is therefore pre-decision: know whether you are downstream, know the nearest route to higher ground, and know what information would make you leave immediately.

## Know the exposure before the warning
Identify nearby dams, levees, reservoirs and low-lying river corridors using official local hazard information when normal services are available. Record the household's fastest walking and vehicle routes to terrain outside the expected flood path. Do not assume the normal road follows safe high ground; roads often run through valleys and across bridges.

## Treat credible warnings as action triggers
An official evacuation order, dam/levee emergency message, rapidly rising water, unusual roaring, sudden downstream flow change, or visible structural failure is not a cue to gather more possessions. Move toward designated safe ground immediately. If vehicle congestion or water blocks the road, follow local emergency instructions and use the preplanned foot option only when the route itself is not exposed.

## Never test moving floodwater
Do not drive, walk, or cycle through flowing water to save distance. Depth, current, hidden road damage and debris are difficult to judge. Bridges and embankments may be undermined before the damage is visible. Choose distance from the flood path and elevation over familiarity.

## Account for everyone
Use the household check-in plan when moving. Assign responsibility for children, dependent adults and animals before departure. Carry essential medicines, identification, communication equipment and minimal water only if already ready to grab; do not delay rapid evacuation to build a perfect kit.

## After the wave passes
Do not return because visible water dropped. Secondary releases, damaged infrastructure, contaminated sediment, unstable banks and official access restrictions can persist. Wait for authoritative re-entry information.
