# Emergency Distribution Point: Queue, Crowd, and Supply Accountability

A water, food, charging, medicine-support, or relief distribution point can fail even when supplies are adequate if the site creates crowding, heat exposure, traffic conflicts or unclear allocation. The objective is a safe flow of people and an auditable flow of resources.

## Choose a site with movement in mind
Separate vehicle access from pedestrian queues where possible. Keep emergency access open, provide shade or weather protection when practical, identify an accessible route, and avoid placing a line beside unstable structures, traffic lanes, floodwater, fuel storage or generator exhaust.

## Publish the rule before distribution begins
State what is being distributed, who is eligible, quantity per household/person if allocation is limited, what identification is actually required, opening/closing time and what happens when stock runs low. Changing the rule silently at the table creates conflict and unfairness.

## Use simple stock accounting
Record starting quantity, receipts, issued quantity, damaged/isolated stock and ending count. Separate food or medical items that have temperature, contamination or expiry concerns. Do not return questionable goods into the clean distribution pile because demand is high.

## Protect vulnerable people without creating a public registry
Offer an accessible or priority process for people who cannot safely stand in a long queue, but collect only the minimum information needed. Avoid loudly announcing diagnoses, medication names or household vulnerabilities.

## Crowd pressure is a stop condition
If the line blocks traffic, people are overheating, conflict is escalating, or staff lose control of stock/accountability, pause and reorganize rather than continuing faster. Use clear visible instructions and one information point so rumors do not outrun the actual distribution rule.

## End-of-shift handover
Report remaining stock, outstanding demand, safety incidents, refrigeration/power state, expected resupply and the next opening plan. Lock or secure remaining resources according to the local environment.
