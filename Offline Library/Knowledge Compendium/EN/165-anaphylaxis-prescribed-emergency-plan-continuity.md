# Anaphylaxis Continuity: Prescribed Emergency Plan, Auto-Injector, and Evacuation

A severe allergic reaction can progress quickly, so preparedness is less about carrying a generic first-aid list and more about keeping the person’s prescribed emergency medication, written plan, safe food controls, and emergency transport available during disruption.

## Keep the person-specific plan with the medicine
If a clinician has prescribed an epinephrine auto-injector or other emergency product, store it according to the manufacturer instructions and keep it with the person rather than in a distant household cache. CDC advises people at risk to learn when and how to use prescribed epinephrine products with their health-care provider; emergency evaluation is still needed after their use. Check expiry and physical condition during routine preparedness maintenance.

## Avoid exposure during shared-food operations
Evacuation centers, mutual-aid kitchens, donated food, unpackaged meals, and unfamiliar cookware increase cross-contact uncertainty. Keep the allergy written in clear language, carry safe backup food when feasible, and do not rely on “looks safe” or informal ingredient guesses. A person who cannot verify a food should have a preplanned alternative rather than being pressured to accept it because supplies are scarce.

## Recognize the emergency chain
Household members should know the person’s prescribed action plan, where the medicine is, how emergency services are called, and which meeting/entry point responders should use. Do not replace the person’s plan with antihistamines, improvised dosing, or delayed observation. If the emergency product is used, record the time and tell responders exactly what was administered.

## Protect against displacement loss
Split documentation from supply: keep the allergy/emergency-plan information in the medical card and backup contact sheet even if the medicine bag is lost. During evacuation, perform a medication/equipment check at each handoff point rather than discovering the missing injector hours later.
