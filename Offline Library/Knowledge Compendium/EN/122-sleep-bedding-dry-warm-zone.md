# Sleep, bedding and the dry-warm zone: protect recovery capacity during a long incident

## Sleep is an operational resource
Poor sleep compounds judgment errors, irritability, falls and missed tasks. During a multi-day outage, do not treat every night as an improvised camp. Establish a protected sleep zone with known occupants, clear exits, ventilation appropriate to the conditions and a plan for night check-ins.

## Keep bedding dry
Separate sleeping gear from wet boots, flood cleanup clothing, cooking steam and water storage. If a room becomes damp, move bedding before it absorbs moisture where practical. Dry insulation is far more useful in cold conditions than extra layers that have become wet.

## Match warmth to the person
Children, older adults and people with certain health conditions may tolerate temperature extremes differently. Use layered bedding and clothing so heat can be adjusted without overheating. Avoid unsafe heating appliances or bringing outdoor combustion equipment into sleeping spaces. Carbon-monoxide risk does not disappear because people are asleep.

## Assign night-light and exit discipline
Keep a light within reach without requiring someone to cross the room in darkness. Do not pile bags or emergency supplies in the exit route. In shared rooms, decide where mobility aids, glasses, hearing devices, shoes and medications are kept so they can be found quickly.

## Schedule rest around essential duties
If one person is responsible for generator checks, communications or care, provide a backup rather than keeping that person awake indefinitely. Use handover notes. A fatigued operator is more likely to misread fuel, skip a sanitation step or make a poor evacuation decision.

## Reassess the shelter if sleep becomes impossible
Persistent smoke infiltration, dangerous heat/cold, repeated water entry, structural noise/damage or inability to meet a dependent person’s needs may mean the location is no longer a viable shelter. Rest quality is not merely comfort; it can reveal that the operating environment itself is failing.
