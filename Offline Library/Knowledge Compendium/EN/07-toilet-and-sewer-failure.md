# Toilet and sewer failure in an occupied home

## Recognise the transition from inconvenience to sanitation incident
A toilet that will not flush because water is off is different from a sewer that is backing up into the building. Slow drains, gurgling fixtures, sewage odour, wastewater appearing in a low drain, or instructions from the utility can indicate a system problem. Reduce water use immediately if discharge could worsen a backup, and keep children, pets, food, and clean water away from contaminated areas.

## Isolate the contaminated zone
If sewage enters a bathroom, basement, or floor drain, treat the affected space as dirty. Prevent foot traffic from carrying contamination into sleeping or food areas. Use dedicated footwear and cleaning tools, and keep the clean-water dispensing area physically separate. Do not use household electrical equipment in wet zones until electrical safety has been established.

## Temporary toilet planning
When a toilet cannot be used, follow local public-health or civil-protection instructions for temporary sanitation. The practical plan needs a private location, a way to contain excreta, absorbent or lining materials where recommended, hand-cleaning capability, and a disposal route that is allowed in your area. Do not improvise disposal into storm drains, streams, shorelines, or places where waste can contaminate water sources.

## Consumables become a tracked resource
Toilet paper, heavy bags or approved liners, gloves, soap, disinfectant, absorbent material, and waste storage space can become limiting factors before the toilet itself does. Record stock and daily use. The Sanitation Operations module can mark a facility as ready, limited, needing service, or unusable, preventing another person from assuming it is functional.

## Recovery after service returns
Do not declare the sanitation incident finished only because water runs again. Follow utility instructions for flushing, cleanup, and sewer service. Contaminated porous materials may require disposal, and severe sewage contamination can need professional remediation. Record the affected area and date so later cleaning or health concerns have context.
