# Mutual-aid board

## Scope
Preparedness is not only individual stockpiling. Nearby people may have skills, transport, tools, information, spare capacity, or needs that can be matched safely. A small local board makes those arrangements explicit without requiring a cloud account.

## Minimal entry
Use a person or group label, the capability or item, whether it is an offer, need, or exchange, the useful scope, a contact method, and status. Avoid collecting information merely because a form has space for it. Precise home addresses, identity documents, medical histories, access codes, and financial credentials do not belong in a general coordination board.

## Matching
Before relying on an offer, verify that the person, resource, timing, and handoff location are still valid. Keep alternatives for capabilities that are safety-critical. Mark an item matched only when both sides have confirmed the arrangement, and completed only after the handoff or task actually occurred.

## Trust and safety
Mutual aid does not replace emergency services, professional care, official shelters, or regulated technical work. Do not pressure someone to enter a hazardous area, transport unsafe material, disclose private data, or perform work outside their competence.

## Cleanup
Close stale requests and remove contact details that are no longer needed. A short current board is safer and more useful than a large archive of old personal information.
