# Fuel & Runtime Log

This log is for planning legal, manufacturer-approved fuel use; it is not a guide to storing or operating fuel equipment in unsafe locations.

### Establish the baseline
Record the equipment name, fuel type specified by the manufacturer, measured fuel on hand, last maintenance date and the real consumption observed during a normal test/use cycle. Keep vehicle fuel separate from generator/tool fuel unless the equipment is explicitly compatible.

### Run windows, not guesses
Before each operating period, write start time and fuel level. At shutdown, write stop time and remaining fuel. After several cycles, compare actual burn rate with your original estimate. Use the worse realistic figure when planning reserve.

### Protect the reserve
Set a quantity that is held for the highest-priority use or evacuation transport. Any decision to cross that reserve must be explicit and recorded, not an unnoticed consequence of longer runtime.

Combustion equipment creates serious ventilation, fire and carbon-monoxide hazards. Location, refueling and operation must follow the equipment instructions and local safety requirements.
