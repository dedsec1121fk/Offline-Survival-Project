# 24-Hour Blackout Operations Board

The first day is mostly about preventing small losses from becoming larger ones.

### At the moment of failure
Record the outage start time. Confirm whether the problem is limited to your unit/building or is wider without touching electrical equipment that appears damaged. Give every person a safe light source before moving around dark stairs or rooms.

### Protect cold storage
Write the time refrigerator/freezer doors were last opened. Decide who may open them and for what purpose so repeated “quick checks” do not waste cold air. If food safety becomes uncertain, use current official food-safety guidance rather than smell or appearance alone.

### Energy ledger
List each phone, radio, power bank and essential powered device with its current charge. Reserve energy for communications and required medical/accessibility equipment before convenience loads.

### Night handover
Before sleeping, leave a paper note containing: outage start, last verified update, battery reserve, water available, any dependent needing a check, and the time of the next information review. This gives the morning shift a factual starting point instead of reconstructing the night from memory.
