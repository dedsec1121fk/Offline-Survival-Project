# Cash & Payment Continuity

Payment resilience is about maintaining legal access to necessities during temporary network or terminal failures without storing banking credentials in this project.

### Identify dependencies
List the recurring needs that become difficult if cards, ATMs or banking apps are unavailable: transport, food, medication copays, fuel, communication or local services. Note which nearby merchants normally accept more than one payment method, but treat that information as changeable.

### Small-denomination planning
If you lawfully keep emergency cash, choose an amount appropriate to your circumstances and local risk, store it securely, and include useful denominations rather than assuming change will be available. Do not record the exact hiding place or account secrets in a shared emergency file.

### Receipt trail
During an outage, keep paper receipts or a simple handwritten transaction log. This helps reconcile spending later and prevents the same limited cash from being mentally counted twice.

### Recovery
When systems return, verify transactions through normal banking channels and rotate/restock the emergency amount deliberately. Report suspicious or duplicated charges to the financial provider rather than trying to resolve account problems through unofficial contacts.
