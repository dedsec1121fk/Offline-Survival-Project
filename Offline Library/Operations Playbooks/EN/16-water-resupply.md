# Water Resupply Mission Sheet

A resupply trip should begin with a verified need and end with traceable containers.

### Before leaving
Calculate the volume required to restore the household reserve and compare it with the safe carrying capacity of people/vehicle. Choose a source that is lawful and currently verified as suitable for the intended use. Record opening hours/access restrictions if the source is a distribution site.

### Route and timing
Select a primary path and a different fallback. Consider daylight, weather, fuel, queues and the return leg. Tell the household the destination, expected return time and what action to take if the team misses its check-in.

### Containers
Inspect containers for cleanliness, closures and labels before departure. On return, record source, fill time/date, intended use and any treatment status required by official guidance. Keep uncertain water visibly separated from known potable stock.

### Abort rules
Cancel or turn back if the source cannot be verified, access becomes hazardous, carrying load is unsafe, the route deteriorates or official instructions change. A failed resupply attempt is preferable to turning a water shortage into an injury or exposure problem.
