# Household Role Matrix

Roles reduce ambiguity only when every essential job has a backup and a clear handover point.

### Assign by capability, not status
List the jobs that actually matter in your household: people/dependent accountability, water tracking, communications, power, medication/equipment continuity, transport, pet care, information verification and incident logging. Choose a primary person based on availability and skill, then a different backup who has practiced the task.

### Expose single points of failure
Circle any role where only one person knows a password location, shutoff procedure, mobility routine, equipment setup or contact. The correction is knowledge transfer or documented access—not merely writing the same person's name twice.

### Handover sentence
For each role, write one sentence the outgoing person must tell the incoming person: current status, next action, and condition that changes the plan. Practice two handovers during a tabletop drill.

Review roles after illness, travel, schedule changes or a new dependent. A role matrix that describes last year's household can create false confidence during this year's emergency.
