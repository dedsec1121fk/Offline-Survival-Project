# Medical Continuity Card Audit

This audit checks whether another responsible person could understand essential continuity needs if the usual caregiver or phone were unavailable.

### Read the card without explanation
Hand the emergency medical card to a trusted household member. Ask them to locate allergies, important conditions, accessibility requirements, emergency contacts, medication names exactly as labeled, and where relevant devices/supplies are stored. Anything that requires you to “explain what that means” should be rewritten more clearly.

### Separate facts from instructions
The card should record factual information and existing clinician/label instructions; it should not invent new doses, diagnoses or treatment changes. Add the date the information was last verified and the source for any critical instruction.

### Offline check
Turn off mobile data during the audit. Confirm that contact numbers, identification needed for care, device details and current medication labels can still be accessed. If a critical fact exists only inside an online portal, create an appropriately protected offline copy consistent with your privacy needs.

Finish by identifying one person who knows where the card is stored and can retrieve it without asking the patient where it is.
