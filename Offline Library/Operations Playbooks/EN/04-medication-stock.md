# Medication Stock & Refill Review

Use this review to understand continuity risk, not to alter treatment.

Place each current medication package in front of you and copy the name, strength/form, directions exactly as the label states, quantity physically present, expiration where relevant, and known refill/prescriber contact. Do not estimate stock from memory.

### Find the shortest runway
For recurring medication, calculate which item would run out first if normal access were interrupted. Mark that date as a planning signal. Contact the pharmacist, prescriber, insurer or local service through normal channels early enough to understand legitimate refill options; do not improvise doses or combine products to stretch supply.

### Storage reality
Record any label-defined temperature, light, refrigeration or handling requirement and whether the emergency storage plan can meet it. A backup supply that cannot be stored correctly is not a reliable backup.

### Close the loop
After every refill, update the quantity and review date in the local register. Destroy or protect obsolete paper copies so an old medication list is not mistaken for the current one during an emergency.
