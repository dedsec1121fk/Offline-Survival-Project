# Wildfire Smoke and Departure Drill

A wildfire alert reports rapidly changing conditions 18 km away. Wind has shifted, outdoor air smells smoky, and one household member needs extra time to prepare mobility equipment. Two possible roads lead away from the area.

### Decide before the clock runs down
- Which official warning level or local condition triggers departure for your household?
- What five items must be ready without sending anyone back inside later?
- Which person assists the mobility-dependent member, and who takes over if that helper is absent?
- How will you compare Route A and Route B without driving toward smoke simply to investigate?
- What short message will be sent to the out-of-area contact before movement begins?

### Complication
Route A is reported closed by an official source. Your car has enough fuel for the planned alternate route but little margin for detours. Decide whether to leave, change destination, or seek an identified safer local option based on verified information.

### Measure the drill
Time how long it takes to assemble people, essential medication/equipment, documents already designated for evacuation, and vehicle keys. The target is not speed at any cost; it is knowing the real preparation time before conditions become urgent.

Never expose participants to smoke or drive toward a fire as part of training.
