# Water Service Disruption Drill

The tap loses pressure and then stops. You do not yet know whether the cause is a building problem, maintenance work, or a wider incident. Stored water exists in several containers with different fill dates.

### First accounting
Count sealed drinking water separately from water intended for cleaning. Label any container whose source or treatment status is uncertain instead of mentally grouping everything as “water.”

### Decision points
- How will you verify whether an official boil-water or do-not-use notice exists?
- Which household uses consume potable water and which can be postponed?
- What daily quantity is a planning assumption, and what actual consumption will you measure?
- Which container should be opened first based on condition and rotation plan?

### Inject
Service returns briefly, but no trustworthy notice confirms water quality. Explain how you prevent the household from automatically treating restored flow as verified drinking water.

Do not conduct the exercise by contaminating water or experimenting with unverified treatment methods. The useful skill is traceability and disciplined use of known-safe supplies.
