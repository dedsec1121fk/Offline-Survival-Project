# Seventy-Two Hour Outage Drill

At 19:15 power fails across the neighborhood. The refrigerator is full, two phones are below 40%, the building water pump may depend on electricity, and restoration time is unknown.

### Build a 72-hour operating plan
- Decide which information checks happen tonight and which can wait until morning.
- Allocate battery capacity to communications, lighting, medical/accessibility needs, and nonessential use.
- Record what must remain closed or unopened to preserve cold food conditions.
- Estimate drinking water on hand separately from hygiene water.
- Assign a nightly safety check that does not involve improvised indoor combustion or unsafe wiring.

### Hour 18 inject
Power has not returned. One power bank is nearly empty and a household member wants to use the remaining charge for entertainment. Recalculate the energy budget and state the reserve you will not spend.

### End condition
At the end of the tabletop, write the first item that would become a true constraint if the outage lasted seven days instead of three. That becomes a preparedness task, not another assumption.
