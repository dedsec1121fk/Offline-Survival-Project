# Return and Recovery Decision Drill

After an evacuation, an unofficial message says “people are going back.” You have no direct confirmation that your street is open. Utilities may have been interrupted and the building has not been inspected by you since departure.

### Separate permission from safety
List the official or authoritative information needed before travel. Then list the on-site observations that would make you stop, remain outside, or seek qualified assessment even if general re-entry is permitted.

### Recovery priorities
Number these for your household rather than doing all at once: account for people/pets; check access hazards; document visible damage from a safe position; verify utilities before use; protect essential medication/documents; contact insurer/landlord/service provider as relevant.

### Inject
A neighbor enters successfully and tells you everything looks normal. Decide whether their experience changes any of your own verification gates.

### Closeout
Write one condition under which you would leave again after returning. Recovery planning is safer when re-evacuation remains an available decision rather than being treated as failure.
