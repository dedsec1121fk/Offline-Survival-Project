# Communications Outage Check-In Drill

Voice calls fail and mobile data is unreliable. SMS sometimes works. Two household members are away from home and the out-of-area contact has not yet been used.

### Build the communication ladder
Write the order in which you will attempt channels, including the delay before retrying. Include one method that does not require both parties to be online at the same moment.

### Message discipline
Compose one message under 160 characters containing sender, location, status, next movement and next check-in. Remove questions that can wait.

### Missing check-in inject
One person misses the agreed check-in by 30 minutes. Before escalating, list what was agreed, what is actually known, and what independent information is available about their area. Do not convert silence into a made-up emergency narrative.

### Debrief target
If every plan assumes a charged smartphone, identify one paper/offline element to add: written numbers, meeting points, radio information, or a message relay arrangement.
