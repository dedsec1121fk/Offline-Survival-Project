# Ten-Minute Evacuation Gate Drill

You receive a credible instruction to leave the area. Assume you have ten minutes before departure and that returning immediately may not be possible.

Set a timer, but keep the exercise indoors and safe. Participants may point to or stage normal items; nobody should rush on stairs, drive, or handle hazardous equipment.

### Departure gates
- Every person/dependent accounted for: YES / NO
- Required medication or accessibility equipment: READY / NOT READY
- Keys, identification and designated document set: READY / NOT READY
- Primary destination known: YES / NO
- Backup contact informed or message prepared: YES / NO
- Route checked against current official information: YES / NO

### The hard choice
At minute eight, pretend a nonessential but valuable item is still in another room. Practice leaving it behind. Record whether anyone tried to expand the grab list once the timer created pressure.

### Improve the system
Move one frequently forgotten essential to a better storage location or add it to the permanent departure checklist. The goal is reliable departure, not a heroic last-minute search.
