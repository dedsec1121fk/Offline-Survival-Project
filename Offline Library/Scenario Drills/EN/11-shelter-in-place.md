# Shelter-in-Place Zoning Drill

An external incident makes unnecessary outdoor movement undesirable for the next few hours. The exact duration is unknown. One room has better sealing, another has the household's only accessible bathroom, and pets normally move between rooms.

### Create zones, not assumptions
Label each room SAFE FOR CURRENT USE, CHECK, or AVOID based on the specific scenario you are practicing. Record why each label was chosen and when it must be reviewed.

### Continuity questions
- Can everyone reach the selected safe area, including people with mobility or sensory needs?
- Which water, medication, lighting and communication items should move before circulation is restricted?
- How will pets be managed without blocking doors or losing track of them?
- Which ventilation or building-system decision requires official/qualified guidance rather than improvisation?

### Inject
The preferred room loses power and becomes uncomfortable. Decide whether that changes the zone assignment or only the equipment plan. State what evidence is needed before moving to a different area.

The exercise should improve room selection and logistics; never simulate hazardous air or intentionally disable safety systems.
