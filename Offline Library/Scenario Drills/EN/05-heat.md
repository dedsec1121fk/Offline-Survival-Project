# Extreme Heat Continuity Drill

A two-day heat warning is extended to five days. The apartment is hottest in late afternoon, one resident takes medication that may have storage requirements, and an elderly neighbor normally checks in each evening.

### Temperature strategy
Identify the coolest usable area of the home, the hours when windows/shading help or hurt, and any location you could move to if indoor conditions become unsafe. Do not assume a fan or air conditioner will remain available during a power interruption.

### People plan
- Who is most vulnerable to heat in your household or support network?
- What check-in time would reveal a problem early rather than at bedtime?
- Which symptoms or change in condition would trigger professional medical help rather than continued home management?
- Which medication/storage questions must be confirmed from the label, pharmacist, or clinician?

### Inject
At 16:30 electricity fails. Reorder cooling, hydration, communication and relocation priorities with no assumption about when power returns.

Finish by writing a threshold for moving to a safer cooled location, using observable conditions rather than “when it feels too hot.”
