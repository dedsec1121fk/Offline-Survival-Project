# Supply Disruption Allocation Drill

Your normal grocery and pharmacy deliveries are delayed for an unknown period. Shops are open but some shelves are empty. Your inventory says the household has adequate calories for several days, less drinking water than expected, and one essential recurring item with only a small buffer.

### Inventory before shopping
Without pretending to buy anything, sort current supplies into: essential and scarce, useful but replaceable, and nonessential. Check physical stock rather than trusting the old number in the app.

### Allocation questions
1. Which item deserves a protected minimum reserve?
2. Can any routine consumption be reduced without compromising health or safety?
3. Which requirement has no acceptable substitute and therefore needs earlier resupply planning?
4. Who needs to be told if availability changes?

### Inject
A neighbor offers to trade for an item you have in abundance. Decide what amount can be shared without crossing your reserve, and write down the transaction so the inventory remains true.

End by correcting every quantity that differed between the physical shelf and the recorded inventory.
