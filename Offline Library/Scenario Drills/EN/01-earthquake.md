# Earthquake Aftershock Tabletop

At 06:42, strong shaking stops. One cabinet has fallen, mobile service is intermittent, and nobody has yet checked the building exit route. A family member wants to re-enter a room to retrieve a laptop.

### Work the problem
1. Name the first three hazards you would check without entering visibly damaged areas.
2. Decide where everyone waits while injuries and immediate dangers are assessed.
3. Identify what would make you leave the building and what would make you avoid a stairwell or exit.
4. Choose a written meeting point in case an aftershock separates the group.
5. Record which utilities or damaged systems should be left alone for qualified inspection.

### Inject
Ten minutes later an aftershock occurs and your preferred exit has debris near it. Rework the movement plan without assuming the second route is safe.

### Debrief
Write the one object, habit, or route assumption that created the greatest delay. Assign a specific correction before the next drill.

Keep this as a tabletop exercise: do not intentionally move unstable objects, enter damaged structures, or interfere with utilities for practice.
