# Cold Weather and Heating Loss Drill

Overnight temperatures are forecast below freezing. At 22:00 the main heating system stops and the repair service cannot give an arrival time. The household has blankets, warm clothing and limited backup electricity, but no safe alternative heating appliance has been designated.

### Zone the home
Choose the smallest practical living/sleeping area, list doors or spaces that can be closed without blocking exits, and identify plumbing or equipment that may be vulnerable to freezing.

### Protect people first
Record who may have difficulty sensing or regulating temperature. Decide how often to check them and what destination is available if the home cannot be kept safely warm.

### Complication
A friend offers an outdoor combustion heater for use inside. The correct drill response is to reject an appliance not rated for indoor use and continue with safe relocation/thermal-conservation options.

### Review
What one item in your current plan falsely depends on mains power? Change that item before the next winter event.
