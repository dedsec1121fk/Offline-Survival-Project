# Flood Route Decision Exercise

Heavy rain has continued overnight. A familiar low road is collecting water, a bridge farther away has no recent status report, and school/work locations are on opposite sides of the area.

### Map decisions on paper
Draw your normal route, one higher-ground alternative, and a point beyond which you will not continue without verified access. Mark places where rising water could trap a vehicle between two low sections.

### Questions
1. Who has authority to cancel a routine trip before an official road closure appears?
2. Which information source can confirm road or bridge status without relying on social-media photos?
3. Where would the household regroup if people are already separated?
4. What supplies should stay with each person rather than in one vehicle?

### Change in conditions
A driver ahead turns around and says water is “probably shallow enough.” Treat that statement as unverified. State what you do next and what evidence would be sufficient to change the decision.

The drill succeeds if participants can abandon the normal route early. Do not enter floodwater on foot or by vehicle to test depth.
