# Wildfire pre-evacuation staging manual

## The objective is earlier movement, not heroic last-minute packing
When wildfire risk rises, reduce the number of decisions that still need to be made after an official warning. Place medicines, identification, keys, communication equipment, protective clothing, water, animal equipment and essential accessibility items where they can leave quickly. Keep the vehicle oriented for departure if this is safe and lawful.

## Trigger ladder
Write several trigger levels: elevated local risk, visible smoke or nearby fire report, official warning, evacuation recommendation, and evacuation order. For each level specify only a few actions. This prevents every household member from inventing a different threshold under stress. Official 112 alerts and instructions from Greek authorities take priority over the local ladder.

## Route awareness
A single route is a vulnerability. Identify alternatives before smoke, traffic or fire closes visibility. Do not treat an offline schematic route as proof a road is open. Use official information and direct safe observations. Avoid stopping on a narrow access route merely to watch the fire or take photographs.

## Smoke and indoor air
If authorities have not ordered evacuation and sheltering is appropriate, reduce smoke entry, limit indoor particle generation and use suitable filtration if available. People with respiratory or cardiovascular disease, children, older adults and pregnant people may need additional planning. Do not allow air-quality concerns to become a reason to ignore an evacuation order.

## Animal movement
Load carriers, leads, identification, water and food before the last moment. Animals detect stress and may hide. A service animal requires both ordinary animal supplies and continuity of its working role. Record which person handles each animal so the task is not duplicated or forgotten.

## Departure check
Before leaving, confirm people, animals, medicines, keys, phones, documents and the intended route. Do not delay departure to collect low-priority property once conditions or official instructions say to move.
