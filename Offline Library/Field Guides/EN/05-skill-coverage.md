# Skill coverage instead of assumed competence

A household can own equipment without having more than one person who knows how to use it. The skill matrix is meant to expose that single-person dependency before an emergency does.

Record a skill after a real demonstration or drill. “Practised” means the person has performed the task in a controlled setting. “Confident” should be based on recent successful practice, not job title or self-description. “Can teach / supervise” is useful when the person can explain limits, observe another person and correct mistakes safely.

Look especially for important skills held by only one person. The goal is not to make everyone an expert. The goal is to ensure that essential routines such as shutting off a known utility safely, using communications equipment, locating documents, managing a go-bag, caring for a dependent person or following an evacuation plan do not disappear when one person is absent.
