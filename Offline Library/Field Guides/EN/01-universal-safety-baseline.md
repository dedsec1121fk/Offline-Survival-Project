# Universal safety baseline

This page holds principles that used to be repeated across many database records. Keeping them here once makes each topic record shorter and more specific.

## Before acting
- Identify any immediate threat to life: fire, smoke, gas, electrical contact, collapse, moving water, traffic, violence, severe weather or a medical emergency.
- Follow evacuation orders, emergency alerts, product labels and qualified professional instructions when they apply.
- Do not enter an area merely to inspect, recover property or complete a checklist when the area itself may be unsafe.
- Decide what evidence would make you stop, leave, isolate the item or call for help before starting a task.

## While working
- Separate confirmed information from assumptions and rumours.
- Keep clean and contaminated items apart when hygiene matters.
- Protect children, older adults, disabled people, people with medical needs and animals from being lost in a fast-moving plan.
- Use the simplest reversible action that controls the immediate problem without creating a larger hazard.

## Handover
- Record time, place, owner, action taken, unresolved risk and the next review point when those details matter.
- If another person cannot understand the current status from the handover, the record is not finished.

This baseline is a preparedness aid, not a substitute for emergency services, medical care, structural inspection, utility repair or other qualified professional work.
