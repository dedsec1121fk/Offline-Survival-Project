# Role Handover Card

**Outgoing person:** ____________________  **Incoming person:** ____________________
**Handover time:** ____________________

### The incoming person must know
- Current situation in one sentence: __________________________________
- Task already in progress: ___________________________________________
- Resource or limit that is closest to failure: _______________________
- Decision that must be reviewed next: ________________________________
- Person/contact still awaiting an answer: ____________________________
- Location of paper notes, keys, tools or essential equipment: ________

**Incoming person repeats back the next action:** ______________________

A handover is complete only when the new person can state the next action and the condition that would change it; transferring a pile of notes is not enough.
