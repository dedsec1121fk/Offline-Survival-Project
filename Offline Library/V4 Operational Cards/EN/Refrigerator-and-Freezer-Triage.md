# Refrigerator and Freezer Triage

1. Note the time power was lost and minimize unnecessary door opening.
2. Group decisions by food type and temperature history, not by appearance or smell alone.
3. Use appliance thermometers when available and follow current official food-safety guidance for discard decisions.
4. Prioritize foods that can be safely used sooner without creating extra cooking or cooling risks.
5. Record what was discarded so the inventory can be corrected after the incident.
6. When power returns, verify the appliance is cooling normally before restocking it heavily.
