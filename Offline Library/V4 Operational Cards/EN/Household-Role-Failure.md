# Household Role Failure

1. Identify every task that currently has only one capable person.
2. Assign a backup for communications, accessibility support, animal care, transport, documents, and other critical roles.
3. Write the minimum instructions a backup needs; avoid relying on verbal knowledge only.
4. During drills, deliberately remove one primary role-holder and test the fallback.
5. Record tasks that stalled because access, knowledge, or equipment belonged to one person.
6. Update the role board after each real incident or drill.
