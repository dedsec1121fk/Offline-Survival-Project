# Route Failure and Reroute

1. Treat a blocked, flooded, closed, smoke-affected, or officially restricted route as unavailable.
2. Compare alternate routes by safety and official accessibility first, not only distance.
3. Use imported GPX/GeoJSON as a reference layer; it does not contain live closures or hazard data.
4. Keep at least one meeting-point option that does not depend on the same failed route.
5. If navigation confidence is low, stop and re-orient rather than compounding the error.
6. After the event, update stored routes with what actually worked.
