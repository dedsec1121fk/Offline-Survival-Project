# First 30 Minutes Incident Board

1. Write the time and the first verified fact before making a long narrative.
2. Account for people and immediate life-safety issues first.
3. List utilities, access, communications, and transport as known/unknown/failed.
4. Write the next three decisions with an owner and review time.
5. Keep assumptions visibly separate from observations.
6. At the 30-minute review, close completed actions and replace stale assumptions with evidence.
