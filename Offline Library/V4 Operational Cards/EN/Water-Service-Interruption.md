# Water Service Interruption

1. Confirm whether the interruption is local to the building, neighborhood, or wider system.
2. Protect known clean water first; label containers by intended use if helpful.
3. Separate drinking/cooking needs from cleaning and toilet needs in your plan.
4. Track actual household use in the Resource Endurance board instead of relying on memory.
5. If officials issue a boil-water or do-not-use notice, follow the exact notice; those instructions override this card.
6. Record restoration time and rotate any emergency stock that was opened.
