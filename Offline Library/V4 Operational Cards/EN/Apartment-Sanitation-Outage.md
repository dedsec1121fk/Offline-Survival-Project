# Apartment Sanitation Outage

1. Identify exactly what has failed: water supply, sewer service, building pumps, or only one fixture.
2. Prevent cross-contamination by separating waste handling from food and clean-water areas.
3. Use the Sanitation Consumables planner with your own household assumptions.
4. Keep hand-cleaning capability at the point where it is actually needed.
5. Do not improvise chemical mixtures; follow product labels and local public-health instructions.
6. When service returns, inspect for backups, leaks, or contamination before normal use resumes.
