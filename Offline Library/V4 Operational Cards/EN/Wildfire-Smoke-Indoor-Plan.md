# Wildfire Smoke Indoor Plan

1. Follow official air-quality and evacuation information; indoor sheltering is not a substitute for an evacuation order.
2. Reduce unnecessary entry of outdoor smoke while maintaining safe ventilation requirements for appliances and occupants.
3. Designate the cleanest practical room and minimize particle-generating indoor activities.
4. Track power needs for any filtration equipment in the Power Budget.
5. Keep evacuation bags and transport readiness active because conditions can change quickly.
6. If symptoms or conditions become concerning, use qualified medical/emergency guidance rather than relying on this card.
