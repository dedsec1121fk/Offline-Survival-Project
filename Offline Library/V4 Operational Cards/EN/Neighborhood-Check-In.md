# Neighborhood Check-In

1. Keep the scope voluntary and focused on welfare, information, and practical assistance.
2. Use a simple status vocabulary so messages do not require interpretation.
3. Do not publish sensitive medical, identity, or location details beyond what is necessary.
4. Record who still needs contact rather than assuming silence means safety.
5. Route emergencies to appropriate services when available instead of turning the group into an improvised emergency service.
6. Close the check-in cycle by confirming when everyone is accounted for or handed off.
