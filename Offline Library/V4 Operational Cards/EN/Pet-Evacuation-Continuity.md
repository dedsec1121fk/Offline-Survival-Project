# Pet Evacuation Continuity

1. Keep carriers, leads, identification, food, water, cleanup supplies, and medication records together.
2. Practice loading or securing animals before an emergency when stress is lower.
3. Identify destinations that can actually accept the animal; do not assume every shelter or hotel can.
4. Assign an animal handler and a backup household member.
5. During departure, secure animals before opening external doors or vehicle doors.
6. Log feeding, medication transcription, and location changes when the routine is disrupted.
