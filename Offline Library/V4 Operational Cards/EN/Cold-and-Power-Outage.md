# Cold and Power Outage

1. Choose a smaller safe living zone that can be managed without unsafe heating practices.
2. Layer clothing and bedding before conditions become severe rather than waiting until people are already very cold.
3. Protect critical water/equipment systems from freezing when safe and appropriate.
4. Never use outdoor combustion equipment indoors or in attached spaces.
5. Keep ventilation and carbon-monoxide safety requirements intact for approved heating equipment.
6. Use official shelter/warming information or emergency help if the home can no longer remain safely habitable.
