# Accessible Evacuation Continuity

1. List mobility, communication, sensory, cognitive, and equipment dependencies before an emergency.
2. Assign both a primary helper and a backup; avoid a plan that depends on one unavailable person.
3. Keep charging, spare parts, written instructions, and device identifiers with the relevant equipment.
4. Test whether the planned route is actually usable with the mobility or accessibility equipment involved.
5. Give extra departure time to accessibility tasks rather than removing them from the plan.
6. After every drill, record the specific barrier that slowed or blocked the process.
