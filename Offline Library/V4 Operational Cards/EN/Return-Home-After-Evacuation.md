# Return Home After Evacuation

1. Wait for official re-entry permission where authorities control access.
2. Approach with an alternate plan; utilities, roads, doors, lifts, or communications may still be unavailable.
3. Observe structural, electrical, fire, water, gas, contamination, or animal hazards before normal occupancy.
4. Photograph or document conditions only when doing so does not delay safety actions.
5. Restore one critical system at a time and verify it before depending on it.
6. Update inventory, maintenance tasks, and recovery priorities from what you actually found.
