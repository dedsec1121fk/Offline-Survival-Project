# Recovery Priorities After Disruption

1. Stabilize safety and essential services before cosmetic cleanup or replacement purchases.
2. List what is unavailable, degraded, and fully functional instead of treating the whole system as failed.
3. Restore dependencies in order: a downstream service is not useful if its prerequisite remains broken.
4. Use photos, logs, serials, receipts, or document metadata where legitimately needed for recovery.
5. Schedule follow-up maintenance for temporary repairs or workarounds.
6. Capture one change to the preparedness plan while the failure is still fresh.
