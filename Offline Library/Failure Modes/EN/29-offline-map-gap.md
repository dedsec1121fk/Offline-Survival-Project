# Failure Mode 29: Offline map coverage gap

## What has changed
The stored or printed map does not cover a newly required destination, detour or evacuation area, so normal offline navigation is incomplete.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check map tile and printed map against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving route sheet, landmark or coordinate before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether scale bar and alternate road provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on map tile or route sheet.
2. Use scale bar as the first alternate only after checking that meeting point does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with compass bearing and set a new review point around map boundary so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For offline map coverage gap, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving map tile, landmark or meeting point as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after map tile and route sheet are usable again, the workaround involving scale bar has been reviewed, and the record for compass bearing reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: map tile, printed map, route sheet, landmark, coordinate, scale bar, alternate road, meeting point, compass bearing, map boundary.
