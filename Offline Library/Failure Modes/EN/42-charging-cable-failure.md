# Failure Mode 42: Critical charging cable or adapter failure

## What has changed
A phone, radio, light or medical-support device still has a power source available but its required cable or adapter can no longer deliver charge.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check usb cable and adapter against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving connector, spare lead or charging port before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether power bank and device label provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on usb cable or connector.
2. Use power bank as the first alternate only after checking that cable damage does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with voltage match and set a new review point around shared charger so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For critical charging cable or adapter failure, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving usb cable, spare lead or cable damage as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after usb cable and connector are usable again, the workaround involving power bank has been reviewed, and the record for voltage match reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: usb cable, adapter, connector, spare lead, charging port, power bank, device label, cable damage, voltage match, shared charger.
