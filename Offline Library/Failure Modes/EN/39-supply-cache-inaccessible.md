# Failure Mode 39: Supply cache inaccessible

## What has changed
A reserve of water, food, medical or operational supplies exists but cannot be reached because the storage location or route is unsafe.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check cache location and blocked room against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving water stock, food bin or medical kit before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether alternate cache and inventory copy provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on cache location or water stock.
2. Use alternate cache as the first alternate only after checking that access route does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with container label and set a new review point around retrieval priority so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For supply cache inaccessible, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving cache location, food bin or access route as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after cache location and water stock are usable again, the workaround involving alternate cache has been reviewed, and the record for container label reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: cache location, blocked room, water stock, food bin, medical kit, alternate cache, inventory copy, access route, container label, retrieval priority.
