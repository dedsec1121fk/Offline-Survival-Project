# Failure Mode 34: Official information source unreachable

## What has changed
The primary government, utility or emergency information channel cannot be reached while decisions still require authoritative updates.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check authority website and radio station against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving emergency number, public notice or alternate agency before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether last bulletin and timestamp provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on authority website or emergency number.
2. Use last bulletin as the first alternate only after checking that local siren does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with municipal desk and set a new review point around verified rebroadcast so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For official information source unreachable, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving authority website, public notice or local siren as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after authority website and emergency number are usable again, the workaround involving last bulletin has been reviewed, and the record for municipal desk reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: authority website, radio station, emergency number, public notice, alternate agency, last bulletin, timestamp, local siren, municipal desk, verified rebroadcast.
