# Failure Mode 44: Critical door or exit jammed

## What has changed
A door or exit that the plan depends on is physically jammed, obstructed or no longer opens normally after damage or power loss.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check exit door and door frame against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving debris, manual latch or alternate exit before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether fire door and accessible route provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on exit door or debris.
2. Use fire door as the first alternate only after checking that door wedge does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with building contact and set a new review point around egress check so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For critical door or exit jammed, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving exit door, manual latch or door wedge as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after exit door and debris are usable again, the workaround involving fire door has been reviewed, and the record for building contact reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: exit door, door frame, debris, manual latch, alternate exit, fire door, accessible route, door wedge, building contact, egress check.
