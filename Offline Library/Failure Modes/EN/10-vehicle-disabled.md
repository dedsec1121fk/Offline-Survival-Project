# Failure Mode 10: Planned vehicle disabled

## What has changed
The vehicle assigned to evacuation, resupply or dependent transport cannot be used safely or cannot leave its location.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check vehicle key and starter fault against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving tyre condition, fuel gauge or charging cable before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether alternate driver and mobility equipment provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on vehicle key or tyre condition.
2. Use alternate driver as the first alternate only after checking that parking exit does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with load limit and set a new review point around departure check so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For planned vehicle disabled, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving vehicle key, fuel gauge or parking exit as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after vehicle key and tyre condition are usable again, the workaround involving alternate driver has been reviewed, and the record for load limit reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: vehicle key, starter fault, tyre condition, fuel gauge, charging cable, alternate driver, mobility equipment, parking exit, load limit, departure check.
