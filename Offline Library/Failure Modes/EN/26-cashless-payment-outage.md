# Failure Mode 26: Cashless-payment outage

## What has changed
Card terminals, banking apps or payment networks are unavailable, so a resupply plan based only on electronic payment no longer works.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check card terminal and cash reserve against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving receipt, merchant notice or offline purchase before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether priority item and budget sheet provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on card terminal or receipt.
2. Use priority item as the first alternate only after checking that bank status does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with identity check and set a new review point around price record so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For cashless-payment outage, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving card terminal, merchant notice or bank status as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after card terminal and receipt are usable again, the workaround involving priority item has been reviewed, and the record for identity check reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: card terminal, cash reserve, receipt, merchant notice, offline purchase, priority item, budget sheet, bank status, identity check, price record.
