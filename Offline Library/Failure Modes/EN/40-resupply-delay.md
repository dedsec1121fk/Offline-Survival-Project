# Failure Mode 40: Critical resupply delayed

## What has changed
An expected delivery or purchase of an essential resource will not arrive before the existing reserve reaches its planned threshold.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check delivery time and supplier closure against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving remaining stock, consumption log or alternate supplier before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether transport route and priority item provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on delivery time or remaining stock.
2. Use transport route as the first alternate only after checking that substitution does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with order record and set a new review point around next checkpoint so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For critical resupply delayed, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving delivery time, consumption log or substitution as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after delivery time and remaining stock are usable again, the workaround involving transport route has been reviewed, and the record for order record reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: delivery time, supplier closure, remaining stock, consumption log, alternate supplier, transport route, priority item, substitution, order record, next checkpoint.
