# Failure Mode 09: Primary evacuation route blocked

## What has changed
The normal path to leave or reach a meeting point is blocked, officially closed or exposed to a current hazard.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check road closure and alternate route against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving barricade, floodwater or smoke corridor before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether meeting point and route timestamp provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on road closure or barricade.
2. Use meeting point as the first alternate only after checking that accessibility check does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with debris field and set a new review point around official detour so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For primary evacuation route blocked, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving road closure, floodwater or accessibility check as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after road closure and barricade are usable again, the workaround involving meeting point has been reviewed, and the record for debris field reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: road closure, alternate route, barricade, floodwater, smoke corridor, meeting point, route timestamp, accessibility check, debris field, official detour.
