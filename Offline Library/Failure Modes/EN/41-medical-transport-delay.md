# Failure Mode 41: Medical transport delayed

## What has changed
Requested medical transport or assistance is delayed by access, demand or communication problems while a person still needs care.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check emergency number and patient status against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving location marker, access route or medication list before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether care note and alternate transport provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on emergency number or location marker.
2. Use care note as the first alternate only after checking that responder update does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with waiting area and set a new review point around change log so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For medical transport delayed, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving emergency number, access route or responder update as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after emergency number and location marker are usable again, the workaround involving care note has been reviewed, and the record for waiting area reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: emergency number, patient status, location marker, access route, medication list, care note, alternate transport, responder update, waiting area, change log.
