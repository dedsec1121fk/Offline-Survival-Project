# Failure Mode 36: Conflict over shared critical equipment

## What has changed
Two or more essential tasks now require the same limited device, vehicle, filter or other shared item at overlapping times.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check shared charger and water filter against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving radio, vehicle key or usage schedule before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether priority task and owner label provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on shared charger or radio.
2. Use priority task as the first alternate only after checking that backup unit does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with checkout log and set a new review point around handover time so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For conflict over shared critical equipment, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving shared charger, vehicle key or backup unit as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after shared charger and radio are usable again, the workaround involving priority task has been reviewed, and the record for checkout log reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: shared charger, water filter, radio, vehicle key, usage schedule, priority task, owner label, backup unit, checkout log, handover time.
