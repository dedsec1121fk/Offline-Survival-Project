# Failure Mode 33: Rumor cascade during an incident

## What has changed
Conflicting forwarded messages are spreading faster than verified information and are beginning to influence route, shelter or supply decisions.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check source note and timestamp against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving official bulletin, forwarded message or verification status before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether contradiction and decision board provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on source note or official bulletin.
2. Use contradiction as the first alternate only after checking that radio update does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with screenshot and set a new review point around correction so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For rumor cascade during an incident, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving source note, forwarded message or radio update as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after source note and official bulletin are usable again, the workaround involving contradiction has been reviewed, and the record for screenshot reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: source note, timestamp, official bulletin, forwarded message, verification status, contradiction, decision board, radio update, screenshot, correction.
