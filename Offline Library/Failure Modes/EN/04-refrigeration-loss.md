# Failure Mode 04: Refrigeration loss

## What has changed
The refrigerator or freezer can no longer maintain controlled cold conditions for food or temperature-sensitive supplies.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check appliance thermometer and outage clock against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving freezer door, cooler or ice pack before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether discard list and food lot provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on appliance thermometer or freezer door.
2. Use discard list as the first alternate only after checking that medicine label does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with temperature log and set a new review point around leak cleanup so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For refrigeration loss, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving appliance thermometer, cooler or medicine label as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after appliance thermometer and freezer door are usable again, the workaround involving discard list has been reviewed, and the record for temperature log reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: appliance thermometer, outage clock, freezer door, cooler, ice pack, discard list, food lot, medicine label, temperature log, leak cleanup.
