# Failure Mode 31: Emergency document cache inaccessible

## What has changed
The planned packet of identity, medical, insurance or contact records cannot be reached when relocation or recovery work requires it.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check document pouch and identity copy against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving insurance page, medical summary or contact list before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether backup location and access key provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on document pouch or insurance page.
2. Use backup location as the first alternate only after checking that encrypted copy does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with paper reference and set a new review point around recovery number so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For emergency document cache inaccessible, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving document pouch, medical summary or encrypted copy as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after document pouch and insurance page are usable again, the workaround involving backup location has been reviewed, and the record for paper reference reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: document pouch, identity copy, insurance page, medical summary, contact list, backup location, access key, encrypted copy, paper reference, recovery number.
