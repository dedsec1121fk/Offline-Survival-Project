# Failure Mode 25: Pet or service-animal supply gap

## What has changed
The reserve for a pet or service animal is lower than planned or cannot be accessed at the location where the household must now stay.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check food portion and water bowl against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving carrier, leash or medicine before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether veterinary contact and waste bag provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on food portion or carrier.
2. Use veterinary contact as the first alternate only after checking that identity tag does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with backup host and set a new review point around service-animal gear so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For pet or service-animal supply gap, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving food portion, leash or identity tag as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after food portion and carrier are usable again, the workaround involving veterinary contact has been reviewed, and the record for backup host reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: food portion, water bowl, carrier, leash, medicine, veterinary contact, waste bag, identity tag, backup host, service-animal gear.
