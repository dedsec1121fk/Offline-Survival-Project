# Failure Mode 35: Expected mutual aid does not arrive

## What has changed
A person or group expected to provide transport, supplies, skills or another capability has missed the agreed contact or delivery point.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check contact window and offer record against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving need record, backup helper or meeting location before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether transport promise and status board provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on contact window or need record.
2. Use transport promise as the first alternate only after checking that resource gap does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with alternate contact and set a new review point around cutoff time so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For expected mutual aid does not arrive, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving contact window, backup helper or resource gap as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after contact window and need record are usable again, the workaround involving transport promise has been reviewed, and the record for alternate contact reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: contact window, offer record, need record, backup helper, meeting location, transport promise, status board, resource gap, alternate contact, cutoff time.
