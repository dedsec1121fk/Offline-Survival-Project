# Failure Mode 02: Mobile-network loss

## What has changed
Calls, messages and mobile data are no longer dependable enough to carry accountability, routing and official updates.

## Evidence to confirm
- Check voice call and text queue against the last known normal condition; write the time of the change.
- Look for a mismatch involving mobile data, check-in window or meeting point before assuming the original plan still works.
- Confirm whether radio bulletin and battery reserve provide an independent fallback rather than another dependency on the same failure.

## Decisions for the next safe step
1. Mark the failed assumption explicitly and move the immediate task away from reliance on voice call or mobile data.
2. Use radio bulletin as the first alternate only after checking that signal area does not change its safety or availability.
3. Record the decision with contact card and set a new review point around accountability so the temporary workaround is not forgotten.

## Safety boundary
For mobile-network loss, preserving the old plan is never more important than avoiding a second hazard. Treat problems involving voice call, check-in window or signal area as reasons to stop, use an established safe alternative, and follow relevant official or professional instructions.

## Recovery checkpoint
Close this failure mode only after voice call and mobile data are usable again, the workaround involving radio bulletin has been reviewed, and the record for contact card reflects what actually happened.

## Field cues
Use these concrete cues when searching notes, supplies, or related records: voice call, text queue, mobile data, check-in window, meeting point, radio bulletin, battery reserve, signal area, contact card, accountability.
