# Local data continuity

An offline plan is only useful if the device and the data both survive. Treat the application, its backup and any paper extract as different layers rather than copies stored in the same failure point.

Keep one recent export somewhere other than the active state directory. Test restoration before you depend on the backup. A redacted export is suitable for sharing structure or blank planning fields, but a normal export may contain contacts, locations, medication continuity notes and other sensitive operational information.

During a long outage, conserve power before the battery becomes critical. Write down the handful of facts that would stop operations if the device failed: rendezvous points, essential contacts, active decisions, urgent medication continuity information and the location of important physical supplies. The goal is graceful degradation, not perfect digital availability.
