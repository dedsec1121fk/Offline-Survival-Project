# Damage and recovery board

Recovery work becomes risky when observation, permission and repair are treated as the same thing. Keep them separate.

An **observation** states what can be seen or reliably reported: “water entered the lower room,” “breaker trips immediately,” or “door frame shifted.” A **status** states what the team is doing with that information: open, isolated, assigned, resolved or deferred. A **next action** identifies the next safe step without pretending that a diagnosis has already been made.

Use severity to prioritize attention, not to certify safety. A minor-looking defect can still require professional inspection, while a dramatic but isolated problem may already be controlled. Close an item only when the intended follow-up is genuinely complete and the current condition is understood well enough for the next decision.
