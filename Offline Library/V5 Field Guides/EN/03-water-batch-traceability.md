# Water batch traceability

When several containers are filled at different times or from different sources, memory is a weak control. Give each batch a clear source, approximate volume, container and current status.

The status should describe your workflow rather than claim laboratory safety:
- **Unassessed / untreated**: the batch has not completed the method you intend to use.
- **Processing / waiting**: a verified process is underway or a required waiting step has not finished.
- **Accepted for intended use**: you have completed the method you chose and are keeping the batch separate for that use.
- **Discarded / isolated**: you do not intend to use the batch and it must not be mixed back into accepted stock.

Do not copy treatment doses from memory into the log. Different contaminants, products and methods require different instructions. The log exists to stop mix-ups and to preserve traceability, not to decide which treatment is medically adequate.
