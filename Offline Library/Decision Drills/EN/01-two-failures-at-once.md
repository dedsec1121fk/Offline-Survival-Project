# Decision Drill 1: Power and communications fail together

## Situation
At 19:20 the mains power stops. Mobile data becomes unreliable five minutes later and voice calls connect only occasionally. Nobody is in immediate danger.

## Decisions to make
1. Choose which device remains powered for emergency communications and which devices are switched off.
2. Decide the first scheduled check-in time rather than continuously retrying calls.
3. Identify one offline information source you can use if internet access does not return.
4. A household member asks to use the reserved power bank for entertainment. Decide whether the reserve is released and explain why.
5. Thirty minutes later a neighbour reports that power may return “soon,” but cannot name the source. Decide whether this changes your power plan.
6. Before ending the drill, write the condition that would make you escalate from inconvenience management to an emergency response.

## Debrief
Did your plan preserve a communications reserve? Could everyone explain the check-in schedule? Which information source was trusted, and why? Record one equipment or procedure change before the next drill.
