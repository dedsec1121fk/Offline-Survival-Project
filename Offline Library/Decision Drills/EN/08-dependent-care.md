# Decision Drill 8: A dependent person needs more time and support

## Situation
The household must be ready to move, but a child, older adult, disabled person or person with medical needs requires more preparation time than the original plan assumed.

## Accessibility decisions
1. Identify the tasks that only the dependent person or their caregiver can define correctly.
2. Separate life-sustaining items, mobility/support equipment and comfort items so the grab order is clear.
3. Choose who carries essential equipment if the primary caregiver also has other responsibilities.
4. Recalculate departure time using the real pace of the slowest safe member rather than the fastest adult.
5. Decide what happens if the normal vehicle, lift, route or caregiver is unavailable.
6. Record one communication detail that emergency responders or a shelter coordinator would need to know without exposing unnecessary private information.

## Debrief
Update the household profile, roles and evacuation card with the extra time or equipment discovered during the drill. A plan is incomplete if it works only for the most mobile person.
