# Decision Drill 2: A household member misses a check-in

## Situation
A person who was expected at the meeting point is 25 minutes late. Their phone is not answering. There is no confirmed report of injury or danger.

## Decisions to make
1. Verify the agreed meeting point, expected route and last confirmed contact time before anyone starts searching.
2. Decide who stays at the rendezvous point so the missing person does not arrive to an empty location.
3. Choose a time threshold for the next escalation step and write it down.
4. A friend says they “probably took another road.” Decide what evidence would be needed before changing the plan.
5. One volunteer wants to drive the route alone. Decide whether that creates more accountability problems than it solves.
6. Define which new fact would trigger contact with emergency services or another appropriate authority.

## Debrief
Check whether your contact card contained enough route and timing information. Note any ambiguity in the meeting-point plan and assign a specific correction.
