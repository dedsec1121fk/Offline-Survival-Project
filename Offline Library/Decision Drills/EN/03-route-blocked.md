# Decision Drill 3: The planned evacuation route is blocked

## Situation
You are preparing to leave when reliable information confirms that the primary road on your written route is closed. The reason for closure is not yet known.

## Route challenge
1. Compare the alternate route already in your plan with the current fuel, mobility and dependent-care limits.
2. Decide whether to delay departure, use the alternate route or move to a nearer safe rendezvous point.
3. Mark any part of the alternate route that depends on a bridge, tunnel, ferry, steep road or other single point of failure.
4. A social-media post claims the closed road has reopened. Decide what source would be strong enough to change your decision.
5. If the alternate route also fails, identify the stop condition that prevents improvised driving into an unknown hazard.

## Debrief
Was the alternate route genuinely documented before the drill, or invented during it? Record the map, waypoint or transport information that was missing.
