# Decision Drill 10: Pressure to return before conditions are verified

## Situation
After leaving an area, someone wants to return for property because the visible hazard appears to have ended. No authoritative re-entry confirmation has been received.

## Return decision
1. List the hazards that may remain even when the main event looks finished: utilities, structural damage, smoke, contamination, water, debris or access restrictions.
2. Decide what source or qualified assessment is required before re-entry for your specific situation.
3. Separate the value of the property from the safety decision; urgency to recover belongings is not evidence that the area is safe.
4. If re-entry is officially allowed, define what would cause immediate withdrawal after entering.
5. Identify who stays outside and what check-in method is used if the plan involves a permitted inspection.
6. Record unresolved recovery tasks rather than trying to complete everything during the first return.

## Debrief
Did the group have a written return criterion before emotions entered the decision? Add one explicit re-entry rule to the evacuation or recovery plan.
