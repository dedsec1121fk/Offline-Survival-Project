# Decision Drill 9: Conflicting emergency information

## Situation
Three messages arrive within ten minutes. One says to evacuate, one says roads are closed, and one says the hazard has passed. Only one message names an official source, but it is a screenshot rather than a live notice.

## Verification exercise
1. Write each claim separately with its source and time instead of combining them into one story.
2. Decide which official channel, broadcaster, local authority or direct observation can verify the claim relevant to your location.
3. Mark what is still unknown after the first verification attempt.
4. A family member wants to forward the most alarming screenshot immediately. Decide what harm an unverified forward could cause.
5. If official channels are temporarily unavailable, choose a conservative action that remains reversible while you wait for better information.
6. When a verified update arrives, record which earlier claim it supersedes.

## Debrief
Check whether the group distinguished “not verified” from “false.” Improve the communication plan so source, time, location and requested action travel with every important message.
