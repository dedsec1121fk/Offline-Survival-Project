# Decision Drill 6: Primary document copies are unavailable

## Situation
The folder that normally contains identity, insurance and important account information cannot be reached. Your phone still works, but you do not know how long it will remain available.

## Recovery sequence
1. List which documents are actually required for the next 24 hours and which can wait.
2. Check whether a protected offline copy exists on another device or storage medium without exposing unnecessary personal data.
3. Identify which issuing authority or organization would be the authoritative source for replacing each critical item.
4. Decide what minimum reference information should be written down before the phone is powered off.
5. Someone suggests sending all document photos to a group chat “for safety.” Explain the privacy and access risk and choose a narrower alternative.
6. Record the first replacement task that can be started once normal services are available.

## Debrief
Did the household know where backup copies were stored? Were backups separated from the same physical failure point as the originals? Update the document register accordingly.
