# Decision Drill 5: Critical device batteries are low

## Situation
After a long outage, the main phone is at 12%, a radio is at 20%, one power bank is half charged and another is empty. No charging source is currently available.

## Power triage
1. Rank the devices by the consequence of losing them, not by who wants to use them.
2. Decide which radios, phones, lights or medical-support devices should be powered down between scheduled use windows.
3. Choose what information should be copied to paper before the phone battery becomes critical.
4. A navigation task is proposed that would keep the screen on for an hour. Decide whether an offline paper/waypoint alternative is sufficient.
5. Set a reserve percentage for the communications device and define who may authorize using it.
6. Identify the next charging opportunity and what could make that option unavailable.

## Debrief
Compare the decision with the Power Budget tool. Add one cable, charging method, paper reference or usage rule that reduces the same risk next time.
