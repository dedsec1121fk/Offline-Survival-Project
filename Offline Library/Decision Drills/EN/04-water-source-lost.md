# Decision Drill 4: The primary water source is unavailable

## Situation
The normal water supply stops without a reliable restoration time. You have stored water, several empty containers and one possible secondary source whose quality is uncertain.

## Resource decisions
1. Count the water that is actually present rather than using the amount you remember buying.
2. Separate drinking/cooking priorities from cleaning and other lower-priority uses.
3. Decide when the reserve should trigger a resupply attempt instead of waiting until the last container.
4. Record the uncertain secondary source as a separate batch; do not mix it with accepted stock during the drill.
5. Identify where you would obtain current treatment guidance for that specific source if it had to be used.
6. A neighbour requests some stored water. Decide how you would share without losing visibility of your remaining reserve.

## Debrief
Calculate how many planning days the verified stock covers at your chosen household rate. Note one storage, labelling or resupply weakness discovered by the exercise.
