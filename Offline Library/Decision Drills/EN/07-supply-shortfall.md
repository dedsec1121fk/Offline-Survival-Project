# Decision Drill 7: The inventory is lower than expected

## Situation
During an actual count you discover that one important supply is only 60% of the quantity shown in the written plan. The disruption may continue for several days.

## Allocation exercise
1. Correct the inventory immediately; do not keep planning from the expected quantity.
2. Identify which uses are essential, deferrable or replaceable by another safe method.
3. Recalculate the burn rate and mark the date or threshold at which resupply becomes necessary.
4. Decide whether another resource can be substituted without creating a new hazard or violating product instructions.
5. One person proposes hiding the shortage to prevent anxiety. Decide how to communicate the constraint without causing panic or concealing reality.
6. Assign a resupply or conservation action with an owner and review time.

## Debrief
Find out why the inventory was wrong: consumption not logged, expiry, damage, relocation or counting error. Change the process that caused the discrepancy rather than only replacing the stock.
