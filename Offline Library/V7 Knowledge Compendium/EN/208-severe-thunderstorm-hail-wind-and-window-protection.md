# Severe Thunderstorm: Hail, Wind, and Window Protection

Severe thunderstorms can produce damaging wind, large hail, lightning, flash flooding and tornadoes within the same event. Treat the warning as a multi-hazard problem rather than waiting to see which one arrives.

## Move indoors early
Use a sturdy building or hard-topped vehicle when no building is available and local guidance recognizes it as safer lightning shelter. Move away from windows and exterior glass before hail/wind peaks. Bring people and animals inside; outdoor objects can become debris.

## Do not rely on a covered porch
A roof with open sides is not adequate lightning protection. Avoid plumbing/wired equipment during close lightning according to local guidance and do not use trees, isolated shelters or open fields as lightning refuge.

## Prepare for power loss
Unplug or protect sensitive equipment when safe and before the storm if needed, charge essential devices, and place lights where they can be reached without walking through broken glass. After the storm, assume downed lines are energized and keep away from water touching electrical infrastructure.
