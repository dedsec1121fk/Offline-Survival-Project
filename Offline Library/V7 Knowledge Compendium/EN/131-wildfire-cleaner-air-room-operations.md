# Operating a Cleaner-Air Room During Wildfire Smoke

A cleaner-air room is not simply “the room with the air purifier.” It is a managed indoor zone whose doors, activities, filtration, occupancy, and heat conditions are controlled to reduce smoke exposure while it remains safe to stay inside. EPA guidance recommends choosing a suitable room, limiting smoke entry, reducing indoor particle-generating activities, and using appropriately sized filtration where available.

## Choose the room for people, not just equipment
Select a space large enough for the occupants who need it most, particularly children, older adults, and people with heart or lung conditions. Minimize exterior openings, but never block the ability to leave during an evacuation. Bring medicines, drinking water, communication devices, chargers, and essential accessibility equipment into the zone so occupants do not repeatedly open doors to retrieve them.

## Control the particle sources inside
During heavy smoke, cooking methods that create particles, smoking, candles, incense, and unnecessary aerosol use can worsen indoor air. Manage entry/exit as a deliberate action: close the door promptly, avoid propping exterior doors open, and move dirty outdoor clothing away from the sleeping zone when practical. If using a portable air cleaner, follow its room-size and filter guidance; higher-efficiency mechanical filters capture smoke particles without intentionally generating ozone.

## Heat can override the shelter plan
A sealed room can become dangerously hot, especially during wildfire weather or a power outage. The room plan therefore needs a heat escape condition: indoor temperature/occupant symptoms, loss of safe ventilation, official evacuation order, or worsening fire threat can require relocation. Smoke protection never overrides an immediate fire evacuation order.

## Maintain the system
Record filter changes, air-cleaner power demand, spare filter count, door/window status, and any observed smoke infiltration. A heavily loaded filter may need earlier replacement. When outdoor air improves and authorities say conditions permit, controlled ventilation can help reset the room.
