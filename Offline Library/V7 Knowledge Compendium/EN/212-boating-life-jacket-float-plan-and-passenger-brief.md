# Boating: Life Jacket, Float Plan, and Passenger Brief

A boat emergency often happens too fast for someone to locate and correctly put on a stowed life jacket. Wear an approved life jacket appropriate to the activity, user and local requirements, and brief passengers before departure.

## Make flotation personal
Check that each person has the correct size/type and knows how it is worn. Children and people with limited swimming ability need particular attention. Inflatable devices have maintenance and user limitations; inspect according to the manufacturer rather than assuming an unused-looking device is ready.

## Leave a float plan
Tell a reliable shore contact the vessel description/registration if relevant, people aboard, departure point, intended route/destination, expected return and what action to take if overdue. Update them if the plan materially changes. An offline paper copy in the vehicle/home can be useful when cloud accounts are unavailable.

## Passenger safety brief
Before departure, show life jackets, communication/emergency gear, how to stop the engine if appropriate, fire extinguisher location, and the importance of staying with the vessel unless immediate hazards require otherwise. Secure loose equipment that could injure someone during sudden movement.
