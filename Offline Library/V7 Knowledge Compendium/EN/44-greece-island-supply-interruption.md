# Greece: island supply interruption and port dependency

## Map the dependency chain
An island household can lose normal resupply without losing electricity or shelter. Ferry cancellations, port closure, extreme weather, wildfire, fuel shortage or mainland logistics disruption can reduce food, medicine, bottled water, LPG and spare-part availability. Identify which household essentials arrive from elsewhere and how many days of true stock exist.

## Medicines first
Do not wait until the last dose to discover that the pharmacy cannot receive deliveries. Maintain an appropriate refill margin within legal/clinical limits and keep prescription/medicine information organized. For temperature-sensitive medicine, include both power and transport contingencies. Never substitute or ration prescribed medicine on your own because a ferry is delayed; contact a pharmacist or clinician.

## Water and cooking
Some islands have seasonal water constraints or depend on desalination, pumping or delivered water. Track drinking-water reserve separately from household utility water. For cooking, know which method still works if electricity or LPG supply fails, but do not move outdoor combustion appliances indoors.

## Mobility and medical access
Record the local clinic/health centre, port, alternative landing/transport options where officially available, and which conditions require earlier transfer before weather deteriorates. A person whose care depends on mainland access should have a clinician-informed continuity plan, not an improvised threshold during a storm.

## Community coordination
Small communities can share information and resources efficiently, but rumor also spreads quickly. Use municipal, port, health and civil-protection sources for operational decisions. Keep a local mutual-aid list that specifies concrete capabilities—vehicle, generator, mobility help, language, childcare—not vague promises.

## Source anchors
- Greek Civil Protection and municipal emergency information.
- Port/transport operator notices.
- Local health authority and pharmacy continuity guidance.
