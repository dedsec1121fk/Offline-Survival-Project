# Safe water storage and dispensing hygiene

## The last metre matters
Water can be treated correctly and still become unsafe at the point of storage or serving. The container, cap, tap, cup, hands, and work surface are part of the treatment chain. Use containers that can be fully cleaned and closed. Avoid vessels previously used for fuel, pesticides, or non-food chemicals, because disinfection does not make those residues acceptable for drinking-water storage.

## Cleaning and sanitizing containers
CDC safe-storage guidance separates washing from sanitizing. First remove visible dirt with soap and clean water, rinse completely, then sanitize using a verified chlorine solution or other approved method for the container. Make sure the sanitizing solution contacts the internal surfaces, then follow the official drain/rinse instructions for that method. Record which containers have completed the process rather than relying on appearance.

## Dispensing without recontamination
A narrow mouth, spigot, or pour method is safer than repeatedly dipping a cup or hand into the container. Keep the dispensing point off the floor and away from toilet, waste, raw-food, and animal-care areas. If several households share water, assign one clean dispensing surface and provide a separate place for used cups and bottles so the clean side and dirty side do not merge.

## Protect stored water from heat and chemicals
Keep containers in the coolest practical protected location, out of direct sunlight and away from fuels, paints, cleaners, pesticides, batteries, or other chemicals. Do not store a bottle directly in flood-prone floor areas if it can be elevated safely. Inspect caps, seals, bulging, cracking, odour, and external contamination during routine checks.

## Traceability after an incident
If one container is dropped, opened by an unknown person, or exposed to dirty floodwater, isolate that container without automatically discarding the entire reserve. A simple label with source, fill date, treatment, and status lets the household make a narrow decision instead of losing confidence in every container.
