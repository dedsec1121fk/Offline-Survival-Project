# Recovery evidence: turning damage into a traceable claim and repair record

## Safety comes before documentation
Do not remain in a hazardous building to obtain photographs. Once the area is safe enough to access, capture wide views, then individual damaged items, model/serial labels, water/fire lines and temporary protective work. A short narrated video can preserve context, but still photographs are easier to index and compare.

## Build a chronology
Record the event date/time, evacuation, return authorization, utility outages, first observed damage, emergency repairs and major contacts. Keep reference numbers from insurers, utilities, municipality, state-relief offices, contractors and police/fire reports when applicable. A good chronology reduces contradictory retelling weeks later.

## Separate three money categories
Track immediate survival costs, loss/damage costs, and repair/recovery costs. Store currency for every entry rather than combining unlike amounts into one false total. Keep receipts or photographs of receipts, but do not delay urgent protective work solely to perfect paperwork.

## Preserve evidence without preserving hazards
Before disposing of spoiled food, soaked furniture or fire-damaged material, check the insurer/public-relief documentation requirement when time and safety permit. Photographs, inventories and written confirmation can sometimes substitute for physical retention. Never keep contaminated or structurally dangerous debris inside living space only because it might support a claim.

## Fraud and contractor pressure
After disasters, verify identity, licence/registration where relevant, written scope, payment schedule and whether a contractor is authorized to perform the work. Be cautious about pressure for large immediate cash payments or signatures that assign rights you do not understand. Store agreements and change orders with the recovery log.

## Source anchors
- National/local natural-disaster relief authorities.
- Insurer-specific claim instructions.
- Consumer-protection guidance for post-disaster repairs.
